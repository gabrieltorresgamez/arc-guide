## Explanation

The Architecture Tradeoff Analysis Method (ATAM) is an evaluation method with several steps. See the article "Architecture Tradeoff Analysis Method (ATAM)" for the full picture. This article covers Step 2.

**Where it sits.** Step 1 is the ATAM presentation. The evaluation leader describes the method to the assembled participants. Step 2 follows right after it.

**Who presents.** A project spokesperson presents. Ideally this is the project manager or the system customer. They describe the business goals that motivate the effort.

**What comes next.** In Step 3 the architect presents the architecture. The architect focuses on how the architecture addresses the business drivers from Step 2.

## Example

A company plans an online ticket shop. The project manager explains the business goals behind the shop. In the next step, the architect shows the design and points out how it serves those goals.
