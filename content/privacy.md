## Server logs

When you open a page, the web server records each request:

- your IP address,
- the date and time,
- the page or file you requested and the server's response code,
- the browser identification your browser sends (user agent) and the page you came from (referrer), if your browser sends them.

I use these logs only to run the site, to find errors, and to detect and stop abuse and attacks. I do not use them to build profiles of visitors and do not combine them with other data. The logs are deleted after 7 days.

## No cookies, tracking or third parties

- The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or social media plugins.
- All files (scripts, styles, images, search index) come from this server. Pages load nothing from other servers.
- Search runs in your browser. Your search terms are not sent to the server. Your browser only downloads parts of the search index.

## Data stored in your browser

The theme switch (Light, Dark, Auto) saves your choice in your browser's local storage under the name `starlight-theme`. The site writes this value on every visit; if you never pick a theme, the value is empty. It stays in your browser and is never sent to the server.

You can delete it at any time by clearing the data of this site in your browser settings. You can also block site data for this site. The site still works, but it may not remember your theme.

## Who receives your data

No one. The site runs on a server in Switzerland. I do not pass log data to third parties and do not send it abroad.

## Links to other websites

Articles link to their sources on other websites. When you follow such a link, the privacy rules of that website apply.

## Your rights

You can ask which personal data about you I process, and ask me to correct or delete it. Write to the email address above. To find log entries about you, I need your IP address and the approximate time of your visit.

You can also file a complaint with the [Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC)](https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/en).

## Changes

I may update this notice when the site changes. The current version is always on this page.
